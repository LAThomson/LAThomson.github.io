This is a placeholder post — it exists so the blog scaffolding has something to render in the index and as an individual post page. Feel free to delete or replace it whenever the real first post is ready.

A few things worth knowing about how the blog is set up:

- Posts live in `_posts/` and are named `YYYY-MM-DD-title-slug.md`.
- The URL is derived from the slug, so this post lives at `/blog/hello-world/`.
- The blog index at `/blog/` lists every post in `_posts/` automatically — no need to register them anywhere.
- Use `layout: post` in the front matter and you get the date + back link wrapper for free.
