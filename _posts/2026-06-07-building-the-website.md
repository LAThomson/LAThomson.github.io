I put a bunch of time into building this website, so I figured maybe it's worth sharing some of that process as the website's first blog post!

![Screenshot of the old website format](/assets/blog/building-the-website/old-website.png){:.zoomable .img-small .img-right data-caption="RIP the clock feature – maybe one day I'll add it back :("}

It started off as a super simple one-pager built on the [\[minimal theme\]](https://github.com/pages-themes/minimal). I first came across the theme via the [\[Causal Incentives Group\]](https://causalincentives.com/) website in mid-2023 while working on a behavioural definition of deception in LLMs based on structural causal games. I'm honestly surprised the theme stuck in my mind so much that I would come back to using it over two years later.

I didn't strictly *need* a website. I'd made one with Wix years ago for my (now dead and buried) music career and found it was hours more work than it was worth; it collected a bunch of nice photos, had Spotify and Apple Music embeds, but really served no other purpose than for me to feel validated as a proper musician who might genuinely keep releasing more music.

Maybe talking about my old music could make for a future blog post...
{:.detail}

Anyhow, this website is pretty different from my Wix one. This is built in a GitHub repo, originally by hand and more recently updated with the help of Claude; when I built my first website, I didn't even know how to code. This one ties to a career I expect to have going forward; I don't think I could say the same for my music career. This website is pretty personalised; my old one had a *scrolling image gallery*.

Speaking of personalisation, there are a couple easter eggs for you to find on the site. Nothing too big or grandiose. I won't give <span class="clues-magnifier">clues</span>, but you'll know if you find them :)
